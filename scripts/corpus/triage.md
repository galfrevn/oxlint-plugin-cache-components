# Corpus triage

One line per reviewed finding from a corpus run. Review up to 30 findings per rule, then compute the false positive rate as FP / (TP + FP). See the corpus section of [`documentation/architecture.md`](../../documentation/architecture.md#4-corpus-false-positives) for the thresholds.

Do not open issues on corpus repositories from this file. Reporting a confirmed bug needs approval from the maintainer, case by case.
